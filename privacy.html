Reshay Stats — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Reshay Stats is a personal tool operated by a single individual (the "Owner") to view statistics of the Owner's own TikTok account @reshay_sam6.

Data we access

After the Owner authorizes the app, it reads only public video statistics of that account: video titles, view counts, like counts, comment counts and share counts. The app does not access direct messages, contacts, or any other user's account.

How data is used

The statistics are shown in a private dashboard for the Owner. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and retention

Statistics and access tokens are stored in the Owner's private storage and are deleted when the Owner removes the app's authorization or on request.

Other users

The app is not offered to the public and does not collect data from anyone other than the Owner.
